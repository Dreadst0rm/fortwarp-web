How it works

From VPN to zero trust, in clear phases.

A phased rollout keeps users productive while access gets tighter.

  1. 1

    Assess

    We inventory your users, identity provider, applications, devices, and current VPN or firewall rules, and identify where access is broader than it needs to be.

  2. 2

    Design

    We define access policies by identity, group, device health, and application, plus DNS and web filtering policy, logging, and a phased rollout plan.

  3. 3

    Deploy on Cloudflare

    We stand up Cloudflare Zero Trust in your account: Access applications, Tunnels to private apps, the WARP client on devices, Gateway policies, and posture checks.

  4. 4

    Operate

    We monitor, tune, and evolve your policies as people join, leave, and change roles, and as new apps come online, so zero trust stays current.

What we need from you

  • An admin contact for your identity provider
  • A list of the apps and networks users need to reach
  • A way to distribute the WARP client to managed devices (MDM or manual)
  • A Cloudflare account we configure on your behalf, which remains yours

Ready to retire the VPN?

Tell us about your users, apps, and devices. We will map out a zero trust rollout that fits.
