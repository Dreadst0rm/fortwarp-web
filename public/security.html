Security

Responsible disclosure.

Found a vulnerability in fortwarp.com? We take it seriously and want to hear from you.

How to report

Email hello@fortwarp.com with a description of the issue. Include the affected URL, what you observed, and the steps to reproduce it if you have them.

If you prefer not to send details in clear text, encrypt your report for our security contact key:

PGP public key: /.well-known/pgp-key.txt
Fingerprint: EF80 50E2 2EF3 1056 1A4B 500E CBC2 9ECD B5AF 020F

In scope

  • The fortwarp.com website and its contact form API
  • Misconfigurations in our setup, including how we use the Cloudflare platform for this site

Out of scope

  • The Cloudflare platform itself — please report those to Cloudflare's bug bounty program
  • Third-party services we link to or integrate with
  • Other Efortune LLC websites and properties
  • Issues that require physical access to our equipment

What we ask of you

  • Do not access data belonging to other users or customers
  • Do not cause disruption to service for other visitors
  • Do not disclose the issue publicly until we have fixed it and announced the fix

What we commit to

  • We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith under this policy
  • We acknowledge reports within five business days
  • We work on fixes with a target of thirty days and keep you informed
  • We coordinate with you on the timing of any public disclosure

FortWarp does not currently run a paid bug bounty program.
