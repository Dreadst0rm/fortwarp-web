About

Built by an enterprise architect.

FortWarp is a managed zero trust network access service from Efortune LLC, based in Bartlett, Illinois.

FortWarp was founded by Ed Fortune, who has more than 20 years of experience in enterprise architecture. His work spans identity and access management, including Microsoft Entra ID, network micro-segmentation, and secure adoption of AI. FortWarp brings that experience to organizations that want the benefits of zero trust without building a dedicated team to run it.

Our approach

  • Identity first: access decisions start with who the user is and what they need.
  • Least privilege: users reach specific apps, not entire networks.
  • You own it: your Cloudflare account and policies stay yours.
  • Plain language: clear documentation and recommendations, no jargon walls.
